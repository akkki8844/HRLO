# Informed Consent — HRLO Study

## Study Title
Human Reasoning Latency Oscilloscope (HRLO)

## Purpose of the Study
You are being invited to participate in a research study that examines how human reasoning unfolds over time while solving problems. The study focuses on **reasoning patterns and timing**, not intelligence, grades, or ability.

## What You Will Be Asked to Do
- Wear a non-invasive EEG headset that records brain activity from the scalp
- Solve simple problems step by step
- Press a button after each reasoning step
- Complete one or more short problem-solving sessions

The total participation time will not exceed the duration specified by the researcher.

## Data Collected
The study will collect:
- Button press timing
- EEG signals
- Session metadata (time, condition, trial number)

No audio, video, or personal identifying information is recorded.

## Risks and Discomforts
- The EEG device is non-invasive and safe
- Minor discomfort from wearing the headset may occur
- You may stop the experiment at any time if uncomfortable

There are no known long-term risks associated with participation.

## Benefits
There is no direct personal benefit.  
Your participation contributes to research on understanding how human reasoning changes under different conditions.

## Privacy and Confidentiality
- All data is anonymized
- Participants are identified only by a code
- Data will be used strictly for research and analysis
- Results may be presented publicly, but no individual will be identifiable

## Voluntary Participation
Your participation is entirely voluntary.
- You may refuse to participate
- You may withdraw at any time without penalty
- Withdrawing will not affect you in any way

## Contact Information
If you have questions about the study or your rights as a participant, you may contact the researcher directly.

## Consent Statement
By participating in this study, you confirm that:
- You understand the purpose of the study
- You understand what participation involves
- You consent voluntarily to take part
